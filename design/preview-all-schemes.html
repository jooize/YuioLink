YuioLink · design note 24 · interactive

The full cast — every scheme in its final dress

What this page is. Twenty-three rounds settled the rules; this note applies them to every scheme at once so the whole cast can be judged side by side before the build. Nothing here re-opens a settled question. Three things on this page are new and marked where they appear:
  • Your "to" question, answered with slices (section 3). Round 22 left multi-recipient editing at "not rows, not optional". You asked for something smarter, and the slice costume turns out to already contain it: with two or more recipients, each address becomes a verbatim slice — sales@example.com, then ,support@example.com wearing its true comma — with the same grey native checkbox as every removable row. The old reason recipients could not be rows was the empty key column, and costume C abolished columns. Two special rules keep it honest: no floor at all — the Draft rule: RFC 6068 allows a to-less, cc-only, even address-less mailto (a bare compose opens), so every address is a removable slice and nothing ever locks; empty the list entirely and the button offers "Draft a Message" — the rebuilt link still opens a compose, with whatever subject or body survives. The headline stays the untouched record; the strikethrough, the button count, and "After your edits" carry the editing. Try it live below.
  • Parts unfold themselves when a warning fired. Reading rounds 16 and 20–22 together: the fold stays the offer on a clean everyday link (closed, one quiet line), but a URL that earned a warn chip arrives with its slices already laid flat — a warning should never require a click to understand. Clean = folded, flagged = open. Needs your confirmation.
  • Checkboxes reach every keyed tail, one rule. What is removable is what the link survives losing: query params, path params, fragments, userinfo, mailto/sms extras and addresses, magnet dn/tr. What identifies the resource is fixed — host, port, path, magnet xt — checked and disabled, same geometry. Removal only, ever: a subset of an allowlisted URL stays allowlisted.
Everything else is the settled record: blue = you stay, amber = you leave — and since round 26 every action button is a split, amber-or-blue action plus a blue Copy segment that copies exactly what the button would open, edits included — and answers "copied what?" by pointing: the line it took from lights the site's green check (round 27); the accent wash exactly once per page on the registrable domain; bold = a registrable domain (and key signposts); warnings are bare red icon + words, facts keep the pill; describe the scheme, never predict the outcome; one hedge line, once; "Exactly as stored" never collapses and owns an explicit Copy button; no selection tricks anywhere. Checkboxes are JS-injected — the no-JS page shows the same slices with no controls.

4 · Tier 3 — off the allowlist · printed, never linked

An Instruction, Not an AddressWhat is stored here would tell your browser to do something rather than go somewhere, so YuioLink shows it, gives it no button, and stops there.

Expires in 6 days

No control at all — not a disabled one. javascript:, data:, vbscript:, and anything else off the allowlist is printed and refused at render time (the same check the revealed page gets — both gaps close together). The panel stays neutral: red lives only in the 16px symbol, because the red fill belongs to the lookalike-domain warning, where an actual attack is on the page. The heading names the diagnosis — an instruction, not an address — and the body carries both the why and the consequence in one breath: no button, full stop. Nothing here can be pressed, tabbed to, or copied into acting.
