Hidden characters, round 7: a tick opens into its label

Put the caret beside a tick (click it, or use the arrow keys) and it opens into its label, so the text is never drawn twice. The field cannot make room, so there the label rises as a flag over the tick. In History the label takes the tick's place in the row. Every entry now shows all three forms in the same order, and the one the mark shows is red. Click any form to copy it.

  • The form's chip no longer has a Labels step: it says Remove, then Put Back.
  • History's chip keeps Ticks and Labels. The row is already the copy, so Labels draws nothing twice.
  • A run opens as one label: 3 × \u{200B}. In a redirect the flag shows the stored form, %E2%80%8D.
