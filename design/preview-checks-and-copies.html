Checks and copies

All five, applied:
  • Fixed slices get real checkboxes — checked and disabled. Same geometry as the removable rows, and the browser's own disabled rendering says "not yours to change" in its native voice.
  • Grey and native are not in tension. accent-color is the standards path for exactly this: it re-inks the control the browser draws — no replacement widget, native focus ring, keyboard, screen-reader behaviour all kept. The boxes below are accent-color: var(--text-tertiary).
  • "After your edits" now wears the full syntax dress — same palette as "Exactly as stored", built from the same parts.
  • Warnings drop the pill. A background made them look like fact chips; they are the card speaking plainly, so: bare red icon and words. Facts (Norway, Mobile) keep the chip costume — that contrast now carries meaning.
  • Whole rows toggle on click — without stealing selection. Click anywhere on a removable slice and its box flips; drag to select text and nothing toggles. The rule: a click only counts if it left no selection behind (getSelection().isCollapsed), and a single click waits ~180ms so a double-click-to-select-a-word cancels it. Native selection, native checkbox, both intact — try both gestures on ?next below.
  • No selection trickery — agreed, and it is gone. Fragile is the right instinct: a copy interceptor second-guesses what "select" means, breaks the moment someone selects across the card edge, and surprises anyone who wanted the visible text. Instead: honest selection (you copy what you see), plus explicit Copy buttons on the raw lines — "Exactly as stored" copies the stored string, "After your edits" copies the rebuilt one. One press, one obvious meaning, zero magic.
Space vs %20, plainly. Links are not allowed to contain a real space in most places, so link authors write %20 where a space should be — every app then shows it as a space. We do too. The dotted underline is only a receipt: "this space is written as %20 in the actual link."

Concretely: the stored text says subject=Order%204192. Your mail app would show "Order 4192", and so do we — with a faint dotted line under the space, because in the stored link that space is really %20. Meanwhile body=call me is stored with an actual space character (mail links are one of the few places that is legal), so it displays with no dots — nothing was translated. If you never care about the difference, ignore the dots entirely; the Copy button gives you the correct stored form either way. The dots exist so the readable view can never silently differ from the truth.

YuioLinks expire and are reused, so a link can point somewhere else later. Always check the destination.

The core slices (:8443, /reset) now sit behind checked, disabled, native boxes; the removable ones are grey but fully native (accent-color). Untick ?next: "After your edits" appears in full syntax colour — watch &q arrive there as ?q — and its Copy button hands over the rebuilt string. No copy listener anywhere: select whatever you like and the browser behaves like a browser.
