Hidden Character Controls, Round 3

Both use round 2's controls above the box, with special characters in gray. The entry is the product's own again, with its three ways to write the character, their captions, and what it does. Turn on the long text to see where the entry has to be.

E1. Entry under the controls, pinned while open your pick

The entry opens between the controls and the box. While an entry is open, it and the controls stay at the top of the window as you scroll a long text; with none open, they scroll away with the page. When a click in the text opens the entry, the page scrolls by the entry's height, so the text stays under the pointer.


    





    
E2. Entry at the character

    
The entry floats just under the character it names, over the text, so nothing moves. Stepping through scrolls each character into view and moves the card along, with ‹ › to go on from there. The controls scroll away with the page.

    

    

    







Notes



    
Copy's sublabel

    
“Without” read too much like “With”, so the two states now start with different words and icons: “Includes Hidden and Special Characters” with the red mark, and “Leaves Out Hidden and Special Characters” with the green check. After one Remove, it says what is still in: “Includes a Special Character”.

    
A folded text

    
Stepping to a character under the fold opens the fold first, in both, so the character you are reading about is always on screen.

    
Clicking a character in the text

    
Opens its entry, as on the Text page today: a label, a tick, or a joiner's wedge (within a few pixels, as the product matches them).
