History rows, round 2: the quoted well

Layout C with every row carrying an eye, a Hide All in the head, and the eye choices kept across visits. Changes since round 1:

  • The kind word is gone. The well's edge is blue for a redirect and orange for Text, and a redirect always opens with the arrow, even when covered.
  • The time sits at the right. The link never truncates: when the line runs out of room, the time and actions wrap below it instead.
  • The host comes from the link itself, so a self-hosted links.example.org/ shows its own name.
  • Covered contents show a lock and a word instead of a blur.
