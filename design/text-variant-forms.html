Variant forms in a Text snippet

A registered variation sequence draws as the character it asks for: 葛 with U+E0100 or U+E0101 picks one of two registered glyphs (Adobe-Japan1), and ❤ with VS16 is the emoji. Both mockups keep the red marks for what is still hidden: the zero-width space, and a VS16 after the letter a, which nothing registers.
