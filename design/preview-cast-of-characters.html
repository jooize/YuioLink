Cast of characters — the blue lid, the tight receipt, and Large Type for escapes

Three answers before the pictures.
  • Why the dotted line sat so low: note 26 grew the tap target with padding, and .dsp's border-bottom rides the padding box — 8px of padding is 8px of gap. Production has no padding, so the live pages are tighter, but the fix is structural: draw the receipt with text-decoration: underline dotted + text-underline-offset: 2px (decoration follows glyphs, immune to any box), and grow tap targets with an absolutely-positioned ::after overlay that moves no layout at all. One thing to verify on a real device: Safari drawing a dotted underline under a lone space — if it refuses, border-bottom stays as the fallback.
  • Do adjacent click zones overlap? The slops can touch, but a contested sliver just goes to the later mark in the string (paint order) — zones partition, they don't dead-zone. The real rule is to keep horizontal slop at or under half a character: the hero's mono advance is ~8px, so ±4px of slop guarantees every mark keeps its own glyph plus its share of the gap. Vertical slop can stay generous (±10px) because marks never stack vertically. And if the cast list below lands, mis-taps stop mattering: nothing is only reachable by tapping.
  • The cast list makes note 26's tap-captions mostly obsolete — that was your instinct ("less messy than clicking every one"), and it holds: showing every uncommon character at once, with context, beats an interrogation one tap at a time. Tapping a mark can stay as a bonus that pulses its cast entry.

Placement, and a verdict to argue with.
  • The cast list lives in the fold, above the record. Your sketch had it under the hero; the fold placement gets the same effect exactly when it matters, because every string warning already forces the fold open — a hidden character's cast entry is on screen with zero taps. What it avoids: the everyday %20 café-menu link growing a tile block on the closed, quiet card.
  • It replaces note 26's caption line (variant B retires). Tapping a hero mark stays as a bonus: open the fold if closed and pulse the matching cast entry — a pointer, not the payload, so a mis-tap costs nothing.
  • Lid: go blue-heading (section 1) — same words, same fold, the main page's own "there is more" grammar.
  • Receipt: switch .dsp to text-decoration + offset once the lone-space underline is verified in Safari on device; hit-slop via ::after whenever tapping ships.
