Hidden characters in the form, round 2

Six ways to show what the field holds before the link is made. Each frame is a live copy of the form: type or paste in it, or load a sample from the bar, which loads it as a paste. Every option is built from the Text page's and History's own parts and classes, so it behaves the way those already do:

  • Marks are History's: the real character stays, its \u{…} label is drawn beside it, and the label is never selected or copied.
  • Clicking a mark opens its entry under it (name, code point, count, what it does). A second click puts it away.
  • The chip is History's: 2 Hidden Characters · Labels, then Ticks, then Removed, which here takes them out of the field. Green with a check once they are out; one more click puts them back. ⌘Z works too.
  • The facts are the Text page's: a red Hidden Characters that steps through the entries, a gray Variant Forms for a registered pair (❤️, a keycap, 葛󠄀), and the tile index when there is more than one character.
  • The rules are the server's: the same character list, tabs and line breaks as layout, and a registered variation sequence as a variant form rather than a hidden character. The page would fetch the sequence table (31,821 pairs, about 15 KB compressed) only when the field holds a selector, so nothing typed leaves the browser before Create.
