B · CENTRED VS. EVERYTHING AT THE BOTTOM

Both are one property on the sheet body: margin-block: auto centres, margin-block-start: auto pushes the whole block down. Both collapse to today's top-anchored layout automatically once the content is taller than the screen — the second row shows exactly that, with the same two rules on a long revealed-text page. Desktop is untouched either way (the rule lives in the max-width: 520px block). The dashed arc is a comfortable right-thumb sweep.

On B4: it is as easy to ship as B2 and it does put everything in the thumb zone. Two things to weigh. The bottom ~60px of a phone viewport is where Safari and Chrome park their toolbars and the home indicator sits, so a bottom-anchored block loses some of what it gained — B2 keeps clear of that band by construction. And with the back chip pinned to the top-left, bottom-anchoring leaves the chip alone in an empty quarter of the screen, which reads as a rendering fault more than as a layout. B2 keeps the chip attached to something.
