Hidden Character Controls

Three gaps from your Safari look, mocked against the /demo/text sample. Every box and chip below works: click the chips and watch whether the chip you clicked stays under the pointer.

Turn the hold off to feel today's jump.

1. The Text page

Today one red chip does two jobs: it steps Labels → Ticks → Removed. The gray chip only removes, so the gray characters follow the red chip's drawing. With no red characters in the text, they are stuck on Labels. Joiners in words have no control at all.

A. A switch for drawing, a chip per tier for removing my pick

Labels | Ticks covers every tier. Each chip is a verb: Remove, Put Back, Hide, Show. Controls stay under the box, beside Copy, which they change.


    

    





    
B. Every tier gets the red chip's cycle

    
Each chip names its state and steps on a click: Labels → Ticks → Removed for red and gray, Shown → Hidden for joiners. The smallest change, but red and gray can each draw differently, and a click's result is never shown in advance.

    

    

    

    





    
C. Everything above the box

    
The facts row becomes the controls, so nothing above them ever moves. Copy, now far away, says what it leaves out instead.

    

    

    







2. The create form


The form draws ticks only, since the field has no room for labels. Joiners in words get a third line, so they are counted and can be hidden. A caret beside a wedge still opens its entry, as it does today.





    
Form with a joiners line

    
Remove and Put Back work per tier and keep each other's state. Today, removing the gray tier forgets the red tier's Put Back (your screenshot shows only the gray line for that reason).

    

    







3. The tooltip


The chip shows a state (“Ticks”) while its tooltip names the next action, and the tooltip says “them” for one character. In A and C the chips name their action, so the tooltip only adds what is acted on.

Notes


Holding the height. The box keeps the tallest height it has had at the current width, and lets go when the width changes. Labels is the tallest drawing, and the page starts on it, so in practice the box never shrinks under the pointer. Today's jump comes from Labels → Ticks: labels take room, ticks take none.


Joiners stay in the text. They are part of the spelling, so the joiners chip hides their marks and never removes them, as the server's Remove already keeps them. They never get labels either: a label inside a word changes how Persian and the Indic scripts draw.


History rows follow whichever option wins, at the same small size.
