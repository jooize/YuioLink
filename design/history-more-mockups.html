History: marking where the contents are cut

Every option uses the real stylesheet. Each frame shows four rows: Text past ten lines, a long destination that wraps past ten lines, prose that runs past ten only by wrapping (so it does in the phone frame, and may not in the window), and a short row that fits. The note gives the size of the whole contents, as a covered row does, so it is right however the row was cut. It shows only on a row that is actually cut.
